Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how FLAG-TAG collects, uses, and safeguards information when you play our online multiplayer arena game. We are committed to protecting player privacy and collecting only data necessary to deliver a stable, fair multiplayer experience.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate multiplayer matches:

  • Player Nickname: The display name you choose when joining a room. This is broadcast to other players in your game room and is discarded when the room closes.
  • Ephemeral Session Data: Technical connection metadata (WebSocket connection identifiers, latency measurements, and transient room identifiers) used to route match traffic.
  • Client Settings: Locally stored browser preferences (such as audio volume and mute settings) stored directly on your device via localStorage.

We do not require account registration, passwords, email addresses, or payment information to play.

3. How We Use Information

Collected data is used strictly for:

  • Facilitating real-time match state synchronization (scores, player positions, flag transfers).
  • Enforcing room capacity limits (maximum 8 players per room) and match rules.
  • Detecting network disconnection and maintaining room host stability.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Match telemetry is never shared with third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

5. Data Retention

Room states, nicknames, and match statistics exist entirely in volatile server memory during active sessions. When a match ends and all participants leave, room state is purged immediately from memory.

6. Security

We implement authoritative server verification for all game actions (movement boundaries, flag transfers, and scoring) to prevent tampering and ensure fair play for all participants.

7. Contact and Creator

FLAG-TAG is created and maintained by Ankit Raj. Connect or send inquiries via GitHub, LinkedIn, or Instagram.
