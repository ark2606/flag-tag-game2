Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how FLAG-TAG collects, uses, and safeguards information when you play our online multiplayer arena game. We are committed to protecting player privacy and collecting only data necessary to deliver a stable, fair multiplayer experience.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate multiplayer matches: Player Nicknames, Ephemeral Session Data (WebSocket ID, ping latency), and Client Settings (volume and audio preferences stored directly in your browser). No accounts, emails, or passwords are required.

3. How We Use Information

Data is used exclusively to synchronize real-time match state (scores, positions, flag transfers) and maintain room stability.

4. Data Retention and Third Parties

Room states exist entirely in volatile server memory during active sessions and are purged immediately when matches end. We never sell or share data with third parties or advertising networks.

5. Contact and Creator

FLAG-TAG is created and maintained by Ankit Raj (GitHub | LinkedIn | Instagram).
